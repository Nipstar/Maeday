## 4. Buyer needs (sources opened 14 Nov 2026)

- **Their own emergency list.** Common core: flooding or burst pipes, gas, fire or smoke, no heat in cold weather, no water, electrical hazards, sewage backup, lockouts. Everything else waits for the morning. One manager only wants a call if the property is flooded, frozen or burning: https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/52/topics/570655-answering-service-recommendations
- **Low volume, high stakes.** A BiggerPockets moderator with about 350 rentals reported fewer than four emergency calls a month, about half lockouts (2019): https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/52/topics/756172-after-hours-call-center-for-property-managment-is-it-effective
- **An escalation ladder.** Managers already run one by hand (call the tech every 5 minutes three times, then alternate tech and manager; same first thread). Minimum: primary, retry, backup, manager, logged failure alert, configurable timing.
- **A timestamped record.** Repair clocks start when the resident notifies you: https://contactcenterusa.com/blog/property-management-answering-service

State repair timelines (general information, not legal advice):
- Texas: seven days presumed reasonable, Property Code 92.056: https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/property-code/title-8/chapter-92/subchapter-b/section-92-056/ (AG summary: https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/home-real-estate-and-travel/renters-rights)
- California: usually 30 days, shorter depending on circumstances: https://www.dre.ca.gov/publications/ResourceGuidebook/gb09_dealingwith.html and Civil Code 1942: https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-civ/division-3/part-4/title-5/chapter-2/section-1942
- New York City: 1 Oct to 31 May, at least 68F by day and 62F overnight; hot water 120F year-round: https://www.nyc.gov/site/hpd/news/029-24/heat-season-alert-city-mobilizes-keep-new-yorkers-safe-this-winter

Fair Housing: HUD issued AI screening and advertising guidance in May 2024 (https://archives.hud.gov/news/2024/pr24-098.cfm), then withdrew fair housing guidance documents effective 17 Sep 2025 (https://naahq.org/news/federal-regulatory-changes-seek; https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-25-107196.pdf). Sources conflict on whether the screening notice was included. The Fair Housing Act still applies. Agent rules: same scripted answers for every caller; no neighborhoods, schools or who lives there; no steering; no pre-screening by phone; accommodation and assistance-animal requests to a person; transcripts kept.

Recording consent: California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania and Washington are consistently listed as all-party consent states (https://www.rev.com/blog/phone-call-recording-laws-state). Play a disclosure on every call.

A2P 10DLC approval usually 1 to 2 weeks, up to about 3: https://www.ghlscaleup.com/blog/what-is-a2p-10dlc

## 5. Capability checklist (for the separate Retell and n8n build)

Answer forwarded calls; recording disclosure; triage by per-property rules; warm transfer with spoken summary; outbound call if transfer fails; on-call rotation lookup; retry and escalation ladder; log who answered; SMS after A2P; email summary; recordings and transcripts; non-emergency tickets; leasing FAQ with Fair Housing rules; owner-call routing; 911 deflection; usage metering by the second; concurrency; prompt under 4,000 tokens. Later: showing booking, Spanish, property software (Rentvine open API; Buildium API on Premium; AppFolio read-only on Plus, read/write on Max per https://www.buildium.com/blog/buildium-vs-appfolio/).
