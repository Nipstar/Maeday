What is a property management answering service?

It answers your office line when nobody can: nights, weekends and holidays. A good one sorts emergencies from everything else, reaches the right on-call person fast, and keeps a record of every call. MaeDay does this with Mae, an AI voice agent set up with your own emergency list and on-call ladder, for $197 a month.

What happens when a tenant calls at 2am?

  1. 01

    Mae answers in your name

    First ring, your company name, a short recording notice. No hold music, no voicemail.

  2. 02

    Checks your emergency list

    Property, unit and problem, checked against the rules you set for that property. Flood yes, dripping tap no.

  4. 04

What won't Mae do?

Better you hear it now than at 2am.

  • She won't call 911. Anyone reporting fire, a gas smell or an injury is told to hang up and call 911 first.
  • She won't judge safety beyond your rules. If it's not on your emergency list, it waits for the morning.
  • She can't fix it if nobody answers. If your whole ladder is asleep, she logs every attempt and alerts you.
  • She won't screen applicants. No talk of neighborhoods or who lives there. Accommodation requests go to your team.
  • Texts come a little later. Text alerts start once US carrier registration is approved, usually 1 to 3 weeks. Calls and email work from day one.
  • She's not a person. Mae is an AI voice agent and says so if asked.
