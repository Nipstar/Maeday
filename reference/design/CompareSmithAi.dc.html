MaeDay vs Smith.ai for property management

The short answer

Smith.ai is a general AI receptionist billed per call, with a free tier and human escalation included. MaeDay is built only for property managers and billed by the second, with each property's emergency list and on-call ladder set up for you. Choose Smith.ai for very low volume or if you want a person to take over calls. Choose MaeDay if after-hours emergencies reaching your on-call tech is the job.

Which should you pick?

Smith.ai is the better choice if

  • You get fewer than 25 calls a month and the free tier covers it
  • You want a human receptionist to take over AI calls
  • You run several kinds of business on one line, not just rentals

MaeDay is the better choice if

  • After-hours maintenance emergencies are most of your calls
  • Your on-call tech, backup and manager need calling in order, with retries
  • Different properties have different rules
  • You want per-second billing and a timestamped log for owners

What would 150 calls a month cost?

At about 3 minutes a call, 150 calls is around 450 minutes, which fits inside MaeDay Starter at $197. On Smith.ai, the $150 plan covers 75 calls and the rest are billed per call, or the $500 plan covers 300 calls. Your own numbers will differ: we'll run them on the setup call.
