What counts as a minute?

Time Mae spends on a call for you, billed to the second. That includes the calls she makes to your on-call tech and backup, because those are part of handling the emergency.

  • Counted: tenant, owner, prospect and vendor calls, plus calls to your on-call ladder.
  • Not counted: spam, robocalls, wrong numbers and calls under 10 seconds.
  • Not rounded: a 2 minute 10 second call is 2 minutes 10 seconds, not 3 minutes.
