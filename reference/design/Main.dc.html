Tagline

When it's a mayday,
Mae answers.

The mayday pun stays in the name and this one line. Everywhere else the tone is calm: no sirens, no red alerts, no panic copy.

M Meet MaeThe voice on the line
"Hi, this is Mae, the after-hours line for Oak Street Property Management. This call is recorded. What's going on?"

Mae is an AI voice agent and says so when asked. She follows each manager's own emergency list and on-call ladder. She never judges safety beyond those rules, and tells anyone reporting fire, gas or injury to hang up and call 911.

Persona: an initial in a circle, never a face, a robot or a headset.

Voice: we say

  • "The emergency reached your on-call tech." The outcome, every time.
  • "Mae follows your rules, not her own." Honest about who decides.
  • "Fire, gas or injury: hang up and call 911." Limits up front.
  • $197, 500 minutes, billed by the second. Real numbers.

We don't say

  • AI-powered, cutting-edge, seamless, solution, robust.
  • Invented stats, reviews or guaranteed response times.
  • Robots, headsets, sirens, stock photos of smiling agents.
  • Exclamation marks or panic. Mayday is the name, not the tone.
